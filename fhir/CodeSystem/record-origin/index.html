YourPHR

Code system: record-origin

Canonical URL: https://yourphr.org/fhir/CodeSystem/record-origin · FHIR R4 · status: active · CodeSystem resource (JSON)

YourPHR marks, in meta.tag, how a record came to exist when FHIR R4 has no standard code for it. A record that carries none of these codes is one a provider sent, unchanged.

Codes

CodeDisplayMeaning
pghdPatient-generated health data (PGHD)The patient, or a personal device acting for them, is the source of this record, not a clinician. Covers what the patient types in and what their devices send. Whether a device was involved is said by Observation.device (a Device whose patient is the patient); Observation.performer names the patient. A document the patient uploads from a provider's portal is not PGHD: the code follows who created the data, not who delivered it.
needs-reviewNeeds reviewThe patient stated this, but it is incomplete or uncoded. It is kept, never discarded, and left out of lists, counts, search and summaries until the patient resolves it. Its reasons are in note.
patient-reportedPatient-reported (YourPHR)Retired 30 September 2026, replaced by pghd. Records stored before then were rewritten; older history versions may still carry it.
