# yourPHR Privacy Policy

This policy says how yourPHR, the health records part of this site, handles your health records. The rules for using it are in the [yourPHR Terms of Service]. The site's own privacy policy still covers the rest of the site, such as your account.

## Who holds your records

| Who | What they hold |
|---|---|
| You | Your health records on this site, which only you can see |
| This site's operator | Runs the server and its backups, and is responsible for securing them and for any legal duties |
| The yourPHR project | Writes the software. It never receives your health records |

## What yourPHR keeps

- **The records you bring in:** the files you download from your providers' patient portals (FHIR files, and C-CDA documents such as an Epic MyChart download), turned into records you can read.
- **Where each record came from:** the provider's server and its own id for the record, so a newer download updates the same record instead of adding a copy.
- **Your notes** about your records, kept as something you wrote, never mixed with what a provider recorded.

## How your records are protected

- **Only you can see them.** Every time your health records are read, the site records it in its audit log.
- **They're encrypted** on this site's server, so a stolen disk or backup doesn't expose them. The site's operator runs the server and holds its keys: yourPHR keeps other people using the site out of your records, but whoever runs the server is trusted with them.
- **Nothing is sent anywhere else.** Converting a provider's file happens on this site; your records aren't sent to any other company or service.

## How your records are used

Only to show and organize your health information for you. yourPHR doesn't sell your records, use them for advertising, or use them to train AI, and it doesn't hand them to anyone else for their own use.

## Your choices

- **Turning health records off** hides them from you. Nothing is deleted, and turning them back on shows them again.
- **Deleting records or your account:** ask this site's operator if this site doesn't offer it. Records stay on the site's storage and its backups until they're removed.

## If something goes wrong

This site's operator handles any breach of the data on this site and tells the people affected as the law requires, including the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule where it applies to personal health records.

## Changes

When this policy changes in a way you need to accept again, the version number changes and yourPHR asks you before you use it again.

## Contact

- About this site, your account or your records: this site's operator.
- About the yourPHR software: [the project on GitHub](https://github.com/jwilleke/yourphr/issues).
