# About yourPHR

Your medical records, immediately and in your hands — for free.

yourPHR is a free, open-source personal health record: your medical history from every provider, in one private place you control. On this site it is an add-on, and each person turns it on for themselves.

## What it does

Your health records are scattered across insurers, hospitals, clinics and labs. yourPHR brings them together so you can see your whole history in one place.

- **Bring in the files your providers give you** (coming soon on this site). Most patient portals let you download your records. yourPHR reads FHIR files (the healthcare standard format, including exports that don't follow US Core, such as Veradigm/FollowMyHealth) and C-CDA documents (the "continuity of care" files that portals such as Epic MyChart give you).
- **See it in plain words.** Lab results, conditions, medicines, allergies and vaccines are shown as you would say them. When a record doesn't say something, yourPHR says so instead of guessing.
- **Add your own notes.** You can write a note about any record, such as a question for your doctor. A note is kept as something you wrote, never mixed with what your provider recorded.

## Your records are your right

Under the U.S. 21st Century Cures Act (2016) and the ONC information-blocking rules, your healthcare providers must give you electronic access to your complete health records, at no cost. yourPHR exists so you can use that right: get your records out of every provider's portal and keep them in one place you control.

## Private by design

- **Only you see your records.** Every time your records are read, it is recorded.
- **Your records are encrypted** on this site, and your health records are never sent to anyone else: no advertising, no data mining.
- **Built for people and families**, not for clinics.

## Getting started

Sign in, open your [profile](/profile) and turn on **Use health records**. It is off until you turn it on, and turning it off later hides your health records from you without deleting anything. Then go to [My Health Records].

## Open source

yourPHR is open source under the GNU GPL v3: anyone can check how it works, and it is free forever. The code, the issues and the roadmap are on [GitHub](https://github.com/jwilleke/yourphr). The project's home is [yourphr.org](https://yourphr.org).

yourPHR is a community-maintained continuation of [Fasten OnPrem](https://github.com/fastenhealth/fasten-onprem), originally created by Jason Kulatunga ([@AnalogJ](https://github.com/AnalogJ)) and Alex Szilagyi ([@alexszilagyi](https://github.com/alexszilagyi)) under the GNU GPL v3, carried forward after upstream development stalled.
