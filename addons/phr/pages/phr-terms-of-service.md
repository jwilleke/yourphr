# yourPHR Terms of Service

These terms cover your use of yourPHR, the health records part of this site. How your health records are collected, kept, shared and deleted is in the [yourPHR Privacy Policy], and these terms don't repeat or override it. The site's own terms still apply to the rest of the site.

## Agreeing

You accept these terms, and the privacy policy, when you turn on **Use health records** in your profile. If you don't agree, leave health records off. When these terms change in a way you need to accept again, the version number changes and yourPHR asks you before you use it again.

## What yourPHR is

yourPHR keeps your own medical records in one place: the files you download from your providers' patient portals, and the notes you write about them. It shows them in plain words. When a record doesn't say something, yourPHR says it isn't stated instead of guessing.

## Not medical advice

yourPHR is for seeing and organizing your records. It isn't medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Records can be incomplete or wrong, including in the way a provider's file was converted. Ask a qualified clinician before making medical decisions.

## Your part

- Bring in only records that are yours, or records you're allowed to manage for someone else.
- Keep your sign-in to this site to yourself.
- Don't try to reach another person's records, or get around this site's protections.
