  Your data controls

Connected medical sources (add, sync, disconnect) live on Sources — not as separate cards on this page.

  • Sources — connect a provider; manage connected cards; Actions → Disconnect (clear tokens, keep records), Remove data (delete imported records), or Disconnect & remove data (both + remove the card).
  • Revoke Privacy & Terms (card above) — blocks new medical-source connects and disconnects Medicare-class sources (tokens only; records stay until you remove them).
  • Delete account (below) — permanently deletes your account and all of your data on this instance.
