Save your report

This downloads your medical record as a single file you can open, keep, or pass on.

The file is not protected. It contains your health information in readable form, with no password and no expiry — anyone who can open the file, or the backup it ends up in, can read it. Save it somewhere you would keep a paper copy of your records.

Send your records by email

This instance does not send email. Download your summary here and attach it to a message yourself — from your own address, to whoever you choose.

Email is not private and cannot be taken back. The file has no password, and a copy will stay in the recipient's mailbox — and with their email provider — indefinitely. Send it only to someone you would hand a paper copy of your records.
