Manage source

You control this connection and the records imported from it on this YourPHR instance.

  • Sync — Download updated records from this provider into YourPHR

  • Download — Export every record retrieved from this provider as a FHIR Bundle (.json)

  • Reconnect — If the connection expired or you disconnected, sign in again at the provider

  • Disconnect — Clear OAuth tokens so this instance stops syncing. Imported records stay until you remove them. Does not change data held by Medicare/CMS or your provider.

  • Remove data — Delete records imported from this source on this instance. Connection/tokens can remain (or reconnect later).

  • Disconnect & remove data — Both at once: clear tokens and delete imported records, and remove the source card. Does not delete your whole YourPHR account.
